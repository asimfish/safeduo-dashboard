Original paired/ref20 detail eye was inside or very near the opposite U_R forearm. The archived original images/videos are retained. A new exterior-side eye offset(-0.05,-0.35,+0.12)m from the recorded U_L hand center reconstructs both fixture comparison methods at the same60 original state indices0,12,...,708. No method or time point is removed.

`runtime_camera/native_camera_replay.py` restores full four-arm q/qd, root pose and target, and both object poses/velocities from the original native NPZ data into a two-environment visualization. It performs kinematic/fabric forward updates and renders, with no physics step in the replay loop. Source chunk SHA, source time/env, image SHA, and native link-pose reconstruction errors are checked. Tolerances were frozen before rendering: <=0.0005m position and<=0.002 quaternion component error, with sign equivalence. Actual error is in `camera_replay/recording_receipt.json`. Rendered q and object poses must be unchanged and simulation physics time must be constant. Constructor warmstart is not a new measured experiment.
