# Statistical scope and denominators

Development reports120 target slots (118 distinct specifications),360 native cycles and720 U paths. Fresh validation reports12 reference contexts,144 cycles and288 attempted U paths. Physical per-step measurements are observations of those paths, not new independent experiments. Paired guard refusal is not a grasp success. Thirty-two unknown random specifications have only request rejection evidence; repeated request count is not a physical sample size.

Fresh reference indices are drawn without replacement, four in each of three temporal strata, on one fixed trajectory. They are not IID draws from the26-dimensional arm space, Cartesian volume, object-layout space or policy task distribution. Within a reference, goals and both hands are correlated; later cycles can be blocked by a latch after an earlier failure. The primary report separates admitted-and-qualified paths, refusals, first violations and recovery.

A finite-population hypergeometric calculation could model stratified trajectory-frame sampling if each frame had a stable, fixed outcome under the same physics, environment and complete frozen goal sequence. The [SciPy official distribution definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.hypergeom.html) describes sampling without replacement. That stable potential-outcome assumption is not independently established here: solver variability, clone origins and sequential latch effects remain. No such confidence interval is presented as established, and no binomial confidence claim is obtained by counting288 correlated paths or individual physics states.

Future probabilistic safety validation must freeze the intended deployment population and task-space distributions, independent trial seeds and environment resets, action/observation disturbances, termination rules, risk event definitions, task-success definitions and acceptable risk/confidence bounds before selection. Development, repair regressions, fault injection, diagnostic rejection and untouched confirmatory tasks need separate denominators. Failed tasks must remain in the assigned cohort; a new mechanism requires a new heldout bank. This round does not establish an unrestricted probabilistic safety guarantee.
